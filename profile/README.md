## 🎓 What is Salsabila EduTrack?

**Salsabila EduTrack is a web-based academic monitoring platform designed to integrate student assessment data into a centralized system.**

The platform is designed to connect **teachers, school administrators, and parents** through structured academic information, enabling assessment data to be managed, monitored, and accessed more efficiently.

Salsabila EduTrack focuses on transforming fragmented assessment and reporting processes into a more **structured, transparent, and accessible academic monitoring experience** for the school community.

> 🚧 **Salsabila EduTrack is currently under development.**
> Features, workflows, and product capabilities may continue to evolve throughout the development process.

---

## ⚠️ The Problem

Academic assessment within schools often involves multiple stakeholders and large amounts of student data. When assessment records are managed separately, monitoring student progress becomes more difficult and information may not be available when needed.

At **SDIT Salsabila 3 Banguntapan**, the existing academic assessment process identified several challenges:

<table align="center">
  <tr align="center">
    <td width="33%">
      <h2>Fragmented</h2>
      <sub>Assessment data is managed separately across subjects and teachers.</sub>
    </td>
    <td width="33%">
      <h2>Limited Visibility</h2>
      <sub>Student academic progress is not easily monitored in one centralized view.</sub>
    </td>
    <td width="33%">
      <h2>Delayed Insight</h2>
      <sub>Parents and school administrators may not receive academic information quickly enough.</sub>
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>

These conditions can increase administrative workload while making it harder to identify changes in student learning progress at an early stage.

---

## 💡 How Salsabila EduTrack Solves It

Salsabila EduTrack introduces a **centralized academic monitoring system** that connects assessment management, academic records, and student progress in a single digital environment.

| Step             | What happens                                                                                                                |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1. Record**    | Teachers manage assignments and record student assessment results in a structured environment.                              |
| **2. Integrate** | Student, class, subject, assignment, and assessment data are connected within one centralized system.                       |
| **3. Monitor**   | School administrators can monitor assessment completion and academic information across classes and subjects.               |
| **4. Access**    | Parents can access their child's academic progress, assessment results, assignments, and academic records more efficiently. |

The result is an academic monitoring process designed to reduce repetitive administrative work while improving the availability and visibility of student learning information.

---

## 👥 Designed for the School Community

Salsabila EduTrack is built around the needs of the key stakeholders involved in academic monitoring.

**Teachers**

Manage learning assessments, record student results, and monitor academic progress across subjects and classes.

**School Administrators**

Maintain academic records, monitor assessment completion, and obtain a broader view of student and school-level academic information.

**Parents**

Access information about their child's subjects, assignments, assessment results, and academic development in a more structured manner.

---

## 📊 From Data to Academic Insight

Salsabila EduTrack is not designed merely as a digital gradebook.

By connecting **students, classes, subjects, assignments, and assessment results**, the platform is intended to provide a clearer picture of academic progress and assessment completion.

This centralized approach enables stakeholders to move from:

**Scattered Records → Structured Data → Continuous Monitoring → Better Academic Follow-up**

The system is designed to support schools in identifying academic trends and maintaining more consistent documentation throughout the learning process.
